ABOUT

Who I am

I'm Abdullah Masood, a CS student who'd rather write the lexer, parser, and runtime myself than reach for a framework that hides them.

My work sits at the systems layer — language design, database internals, and the occasional native Windows app built with nothing but the Win32 API. I prefer working from a design document to a finished implementation over scaffolding tools doing the thinking for me. Outside the terminal, I'm just as interested in the human decisions and accidents that shaped how computing ended up looking the way it does.
